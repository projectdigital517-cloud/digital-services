FAQ

Questions people ask before they start

How long does a project take?

Digital business cards take about 2 days, logos about 5 days and websites about 10 working days from the day you send your content.

How many revisions do I get?

Every package includes revision rounds. Starter has 2, Professional has 3 and the Complete Bundle has unlimited minor edits for 30 days.

How does payment work?

You pay 50% to start and 50% before we hand over the final files. We accept bank transfer and card payment.

Who owns the final files?

You do. Once the final payment is made, the logo files, website content and card design belong to you.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

Yes. We can register your domain and host your site for you, or hand everything over to your own provider. We explain the yearly cost upfront.

What support do I get after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free support for fixes and small changes. After that, you can add a monthly care plan or pay per request.

Does the digital card work on all phones?

Yes. It opens from a link or QR code on any phone. The NFC tap works on most modern iPhone and Android models.
